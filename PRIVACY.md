# Privacy

Management Consulting is a static set of skill instructions and reference files. It does not run a service, collect personal data, send analytics, or make network requests.

The AI product you use may process the information you provide. Its privacy policy and settings apply to that processing. Avoid sharing confidential client information unless your organization's policies and the AI product's controls permit it.

Ask questions about this project through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/anotb/management-consulting-plugin/issues).
